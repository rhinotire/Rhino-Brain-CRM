## 1. Project Mission

Build RHINO TIRE USA and EVERFLOW TIRES & WHEELS as modern B2B tire distribution websites that also convert consumer traffic into:

1. Retail installation leads for IDEAL TIRES & WHEELS
2. Installation leads for approved partner tire shops
3. New Dealer prospects for RHINO TIRE USA
4. New Dealer prospects for EVERFLOW TIRES & WHEELS
5. Wholesale product sales generated by real consumer demand
6. Local market demand data by ZIP code, product, vehicle, installer, and region

The websites must not treat consumer visitors as irrelevant traffic.
The platform must operate as a dual-channel system:

```text
B2B Dealer Traffic
→ Wholesale inventory
→ Dealer pricing
→ Quote
→ Order
→ Dealer account

Consumer Traffic
→ Product discovery
→ ZIP code
→ Installation option
→ IDEAL or partner installer
→ Wholesale order
→ Installation
→ Consumer review
→ Dealer development
```

The long-term business objective is:
RHINO and EVERFLOW provide both products and consumer leads to tire shops, while tire shops provide local installation capability and recurring wholesale purchasing.

## 5. Dual-Channel Website Requirement

Each B2B website must serve both business customers and consumers without confusing the two audiences.

**Website Header** — the website must always provide two clear paths.

Business Path:

```text
Buying for Your Business?

[ Search Wholesale Inventory ]
[ Dealer Login ]
```

Consumer Path:

```text
Need Tires Installed?

[ Find Tires & Installation Near Me ]
```

Do not use a mandatory full-screen audience-selection popup on first visit.
The preferred behavior is:

* Keep both entry points visible in the navigation
* Detect intent based on search behavior
* Detect intent based on page type
* Allow the user to switch paths
* Show both B2B and consumer CTAs on product pages
* Never block a consumer from viewing product information
* Never expose protected Dealer pricing to consumers

## 9. Consumer Lead Routing Priority

**Priority 1: IDEAL TIRES & WHEELS** — if the consumer is within the configured IDEAL service area:

```text
Consumer selects product
→ Consumer enters ZIP code
→ System checks IDEAL service area
→ System checks product availability
→ System checks installation capability
→ System checks appointment availability
→ IDEAL is shown as the first option
```

IDEAL consumer page should display: product, tire retail price where approved, mounting and balancing, installation availability, same-day availability only when verified, store address, phone, store hours, directions, reviews, appointment request, reserve tires, installed-price request.

Recommended wording:

```text
Available Near Orlando
Professional installation available at IDEAL TIRES & WHEELS.
```

Buttons: `[ Check Installed Price ] [ Book Installation ] [ Call IDEAL TIRES ] [ Get Directions ]`

The IDEAL service radius must be configurable.

**Priority 2: Approved Partner Installers** — if the consumer is outside the IDEAL service area:

```text
ZIP code → Search approved installers → Filter by product capability → Filter by installation capability
→ Check active status → Check service area → Check response performance → Display up to 3 qualified nearby installers
```

Installer ranking should consider: distance, tire category capability (passenger / light truck / trailer / TBR / custom wheel), maximum wheel size, mobile installation capability, product availability, ability to receive delivery quickly, existing RHINO or EVERFLOW Dealer status, response time, appointment availability, store hours, customer rating, active participation status.

Distance must not be the only ranking factor.

**Priority 3: No Approved Installer Available** — never show only "No Store Found". Instead display:

```text
We're locating an installer near you.
Tell us what you need and our team will help connect you with a qualified shop.
```

Collect: consumer name, mobile phone, email, ZIP code, tire size, vehicle, quantity, preferred installation date, preferred contact method.

Create:

```text
Consumer Installation Lead
Status: Installer Needed
Market: Consumer ZIP
Product: Selected Product
```

The sales team may then: (1) recommend an existing Dealer, (2) contact a nearby tire shop, (3) tell the tire shop that a consumer selected or needs installation in the area, (4) offer wholesale product supply, (5) develop the tire shop into a Dealer.

This is both a consumer service and a Dealer recruitment workflow.

## 10. Core Consumer Hooks

**Hook 1: Check Installed Price Near Me**

```text
Check Installed Price Near You
[ Tire Size ] [ ZIP Code ] [ Get Local Options ]
```

The consumer wants: final installed price, product availability, distance, installation date, store reputation.
For IDEAL, exact installed pricing may be shown when verified.
For independent partner stores, use `Request Local Installed Price` unless the store has approved real-time installed pricing.

**Hook 2: Same-Day Installation** — display `Available for Same-Day Installation` only when ALL verified: product available, store open, installer supports the product, appointment capacity exists, delivery timing compatible. Do not use fake urgency.

**Hook 3: Reserve My Tires** — allow `Reserve 4 Tires / Choose Installation Time`. MVP may support a reservation request without payment. Future phases: deposit, full payment, time-limited hold, automatic inventory allocation. Do not allocate live inventory without a verified inventory transaction.

**Hook 4: Send This Tire to My Installer**

```text
Send This Tire to My Installer
Already have a trusted tire shop?
Send them this product request and ask them to source it for you.
```

This feature is a major competitive differentiator. It must: (1) capture consumer demand, (2) identify the installer, (3) check whether the installer is an existing Dealer, (4) create a Dealer opportunity, (5) contact the installer, (6) provide wholesale product options, (7) allow the installer to accept the consumer, (8) convert the opportunity into a wholesale order, (9) track the installer as a Dealer prospect.

**Hook 5: Consumer Coupon** — configurable consumer offers (e.g. `$20 Installation Credit, valid at participating locations`). Every offer must include: unique coupon code, participating store, product eligibility, expiration date, source website, campaign ID, usage status, redemption status, terms. Do not issue offers without a defined funding source and redemption policy. *(Deferred to Phase 2 — see Addendum.)*

## 11. Send This Tire to My Installer Workflow

**Consumer form** (short, mobile-friendly):

* Consumer: first name, last name or initial, phone, email, ZIP, preferred contact method, contact consent
* Product (pre-populated from product page): product ID, SKU, brand, pattern, tire size, quantity, category, application, source page
* Optional: vehicle year/make/model, trailer type, preferred installation date, notes
* Installer: business name, phone, address, ZIP, website, Google Maps link, business card upload, invoice/receipt upload, storefront photo, "I only know the shop location"

Do not require every installer field. Require enough to identify or contact the installer.

**Existing Dealer matching** — before creating a new installer prospect, search existing RHINO Dealers, EVERFLOW Dealers, installer records, customer records, prospects, leads. Match by: phone, address, business name, website domain, Google Maps URL, email domain, ZIP, tax/business info where available.

Match statuses:

```text
Existing RHINO Dealer / Existing EVERFLOW Dealer / Existing Partner Installer /
Existing Prospect / Possible Duplicate / New Installer Prospect
```

Do not silently create duplicates.

**Existing Dealer workflow:**

```text
Consumer submits request → Match existing Dealer → Identify assigned sales representative
→ Check company ownership → Check warehouse availability → Create Dealer opportunity
→ Create draft wholesale quote → Notify Dealer → Notify sales representative
→ Dealer accepts or declines → Consumer receives update
```

Preserve the existing sales representative assignment unless explicitly approved otherwise.

**New installer prospect workflow:**

```text
Consumer submits request → No Dealer match found → Create Installer Prospect
→ Create Consumer Referral Lead → Assign RHINO or EVERFLOW → Assign warehouse
→ Assign sales representative → Generate product opportunity → Contact installer
→ Offer wholesale pricing → Installer accepts consumer → Installer completes first order
→ Invite installer to become approved Dealer
```

First outreach emphasizes:

```text
A customer in your area selected your shop and requested [quantity] of [product].

We may have this product available from our [Orlando/Dallas] warehouse and can
provide your business with wholesale pricing.

Please review the customer request and let us know whether you would like to
accept the installation opportunity.
```

**Secure installer request page** — `/shop-request/{secure-token}`, no predictable IDs. Initial view shows product, quantity, customer ZIP, requested service, preferred date. Buttons:

```text
[ Accept This Customer ] [ Request Wholesale Price ] [ Open Dealer Account ] [ Contact Sales ] [ Decline Request ]
```

Initially mask consumer information (e.g. `Customer: William Y. / ZIP: 32809`). Release full contact only when: installer accepts, consumer consent is valid, authorization succeeds, assignment is confirmed.

**Consumer status page** — after submission show progress, not just a thank-you:

```text
Your Tire Request Has Been Sent
1. Product selected
2. Installer identified
3. RHINO/EVERFLOW is contacting the shop
4. Waiting for installer confirmation
```

Consumer statuses: `Request Submitted / Installer Being Verified / Installer Contacted / Installer Accepted / Product Quote Confirmed / Installation Scheduled / Completed / Unable to Complete / Cancelled / Expired`.

Provide: secure tracking link, email/SMS updates, change-installer option, cancel option, contact support, choose IDEAL or another installer where available.

Do not expose: wholesale price, internal notes, dealer cost, dealer credit information, internal sales comments.

## 15. AI Assistant Requirements

The AI Assistant must first identify customer type — do not begin with only "How can I help you?". Use:

```text
Are you buying for:
[ My Business / Tire Shop ]  [ My Personal Vehicle ]  [ A Trailer or Fleet ]
```

**Business buyer path:** search wholesale inventory → identify company → identify warehouse → check Dealer access → retrieve approved pricing → create quote → assign sales representative.

**Consumer path:** ask ZIP, tire size or Year/Make/Model, quantity, installation required?, preferred date, existing installer? Then: find product → check local installation options → prioritize IDEAL when qualified → find partner installers → offer Send to My Installer → create lead → notify assigned store or sales team.

## 16. Pricing Separation

The system must support at least:

```text
Internal Cost / Dealer Wholesale Price / Consumer Tire Price / Consumer Installed Price
```

Consumer installed price may include: tire retail price + mounting + balancing + valve + disposal + TPMS service + taxes + shop fees.

Rules:

1. Internal cost is never public.
2. Dealer pricing requires server-side authorization.
3. Consumer pricing must not expose wholesale price.
4. Each installer may have different service pricing.
5. IDEAL may display exact installed price when verified.
6. Partner installers normally display `Request Local Installed Price` / `Pricing Provided by Installer`.
7. Do not promise nationwide installed pricing without store approval.
8. Price effective dates must be recorded.

These decisions were proposed by the engineering side and **approved by William**. Where they differ from the body above, the addendum wins.

1. **Single website codebase, multi-brand by domain.** `apps/website` serves both rhinotiresusa.com and everflowtires.com by reading the request Host and loading a brand configuration record (name, colors, warehouse, navigation, copy, installer-network name).
